# notes

1. GST on Legal Fees and disbursements needs to be included, but GST is exempt on Land Titles and Mortgage Registration Fees

2. For house sales, the real property report is not included (~850) or Condominium Estopple Certificate ($150-350).

3. Title Insurance is also not included in the fee calculation for Buying + Mortgage or Selling, and Refinancing.

4. Legal Fees estimate entries needs to be in a light colored font.
